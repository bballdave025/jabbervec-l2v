Beware the jabberwock my son.
The jaws that bite, the claws that catch.
Frabjous day, callooh, callay.
Krumpets clatter in the cupboard.
Frappadinkochlorffin is not a word, but it knows where English keeps its elbows.
The slithy letters gyre and gimble in the vector space.
A nonsense word can still have neighbors.
